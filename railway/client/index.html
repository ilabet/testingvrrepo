Second Life VR

WebXR viewer for Quest, Vive, Index and other headsets.

This bridge runs in demo-only mode.

Your password is sent only to this bridge server, which logs in to Second Life on your behalf. Only use a bridge you host or trust. Not affiliated with Linden Lab.
